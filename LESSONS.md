# Lessons for KOML

Canary selection and promotion gates are separate from deployment execution. Recorded candidate observations cannot justify claims about unobserved workflows. The fixture validates the assumption that explicit deterministic rules can recover planted regression cases exactly: 12 expected, 12 found, zero false positives. It does not validate representativeness or real-world value.

One operating rule for future builds: preserve a hand-derived expectation file outside the core calculation, and mutate an observation to prove the report responds to data rather than returning a canned total. Lexicographic selection is transparent but biased; any future random allocation must define a stable seed and migration policy.

A production deployment integration and representative cohort allocation remain possible improvements, subject to the two-week real-use findings. No real effectiveness, saved time or revenue is claimed.
